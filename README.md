## Security Engineering Philosophy

I approach security investigations through a structured process:

1. **Observe** — Understand the alert, event, or suspicious activity.
2. **Collect** — Identify the relevant logs, endpoint telemetry, and supporting evidence.
3. **Investigate** — Correlate events and establish the sequence of activity.
4. **Validate** — Determine whether the behaviour is malicious, benign, or inconclusive.
5. **Respond** — Recommend appropriate containment, remediation, or escalation.
6. **Improve** — Identify detection gaps and opportunities to strengthen security monitoring.

I believe a good security analyst should be able to explain not only what happened, but also why it matters and what should happen next.
